What it does

Write. Link. Find it again.

The folder is not the idea — the link is. Vellen is built so a thought you wrote down in March finds its way back to you in September.

Notes that connect

Type [[ and link to any note. The Links panel shows every note that mentions the one you're reading — even the ones that never linked back. The graph shows the shape of your thinking.

  • Wikilinks, backlinks and unlinked mentions

Boards made from your notes

Give the notes in a folder a few properties — a status, an owner, a date — and that folder becomes a table or a kanban board. Drag a card between columns and the note's property changes. The board is a view; the notes are still the notes.

  • Table and board views of any folder, saved with it
  • Filter, sort and group by any property
  • A task dashboard that reads every checkbox in your vault, with due dates

Whiteboards for thinking sideways

Some ideas aren't a list yet. Put cards on a canvas, draw arrows between them, drop a whole note in as a card. The whiteboard is a file in your folder like everything else.

  • Pinch, pan and zoom on a tablet

Why Vellen

What we believe about notes

A note should outlive the app that wrote it.

Most notes apps keep your writing inside themselves — a database, a cloud, a format that only they can read. Leave, and you export. Vellen never has that conversation with you. Your notes are Markdown files in a folder you picked. Open them with anything. Back them up however you already do. If Vellen vanished tomorrow, you would lose nothing.

Your writing is nobody's business.

We don't run servers, so there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to leak. Sync, if you turn it on, goes through your cloud drive, and you can seal it with end-to-end encryption before it leaves your device. No analytics, no telemetry, no model trained on your journal.

Structure should come from the writing, not before it.

You shouldn't have to decide where a thought lives before you've had it. Write first. Link when a connection occurs to you. Add a property when a folder starts to look like a list. The board, the graph and the task list are views over what you already wrote — never a separate thing to maintain.

Buy it once.

A notes app is something you trust with years of thinking. It shouldn't stop working the month you cancel. Vellen is a one-time purchase, with a full-featured trial first so you can decide with your own notes in it.

Sync & privacy

Your Drive, not our server

Connect your own Google Drive and your notes follow you to every device. We never see them: there is no Vellen server for them to pass through.

  1. Sign in to your own Google account. Vellen asks for Google's narrowest Drive permission — it can see and manage only the folder it creates, never the rest of your Drive.
  2. Turn on end-to-end encryption, if you like. Notes are sealed on your device with a passphrase only you know before they upload. Not even Google can read them.
  3. Write anywhere. Edits on one device arrive on the others. If two devices change the same note, both versions are kept — Vellen never silently picks one.

Or skip sync entirely. Vellen is complete without it.

What we promise

  • No account with us — there is nothing to create.
  • No servers. Sync is a conversation between your devices and your Drive.
  • No data collected. No analytics, no crash telemetry, no usage tracking.
  • No training. Your notes are never used to train anything.
  • Data-loss guards. Sync refuses to act on a Drive that suddenly looks empty, asks before removing several notes, and keeps conflict copies rather than overwriting.
  • The privacy policy is short and means it.

How it's different

Most apps put AI on a subscription and route your notes through their own service to do it. Vellen has no service. Your key goes to your provider, your notes go with the question you asked, and the answer comes back to a file on your disk. Change providers in a menu. Turn it off and it's gone.

Questions

The ones people ask first

Can I use the notes I already have?

If they're Markdown files, yes — point Vellen at the folder and they're your vault, links and all. Most notes apps can export to Markdown; once yours has, you're done.

Do I have to use sync?

No. Vellen is complete on one device. Sync exists for people with a Mac and a phone, and it goes through your own Google Drive, never through us.

What happens to my notes if I stop using Vellen?

Nothing. They're Markdown files in a folder you chose. Whiteboards are JSON files beside them. Open them in any editor or in another app that reads Markdown.

Is there a subscription?

No. One purchase, per store, forever. Updates are free. The trial is the full app for two weeks.

Does the AI see my notes?

Only the provider you chose, only when you ask, and only the notes involved in the question. Vellen has no service in the middle. Use a local model and nothing leaves your machine.

What does "end-to-end encrypted" mean here?

Your notes are sealed on your device with a passphrase only you know, before they upload to your Drive. Google stores files it can't read. The copies on your own devices stay plain, so forgetting the passphrase costs you nothing locally — you get a recovery phrase too, and any device that still has your notes can rebuild the Drive copy. Nobody can open the sealed copy for you, including us. That's the point.
