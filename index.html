Vellen

Notes that connect. Yours, in a folder you choose, forever.

Vellen is a notes app built on one conviction: your notes should be plain files you own — readable by any app, in a folder you picked, on your own device. Everything else is built on top of that, never instead of it.

  • Plain Markdown files. No proprietary database, no lock-in. Your vault opens in any text editor.
  • Notes that link. Wikilinks, backlinks, embeds, tags, and a graph of how your thinking connects.
  • More than text. Whiteboards for spatial thinking, boards for tasks, daily notes for the everyday.
  • Private sync, if you want it. Optional sync through your own Google Drive with end-to-end encryption — we run no servers and can't read your notes. Or skip it entirely.
  • No account. No tracking. No training. The details are in the privacy policy — it's short and it means it.

Where it runs

macOS, Windows, iPhone, iPad, and Android.
