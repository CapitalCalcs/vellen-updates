Vellen — What's new

Latest: version 1.0.37, September 18, 2026

Every version, what it changed, and what was wrong before it. Written plainly, on purpose: a notes app is something you trust with your thinking, and you should be able to see exactly what we fixed and when. Something not right? Write to admin@capitalcalcs.com, or use Settings → About → Report a problem inside Vellen.

Version 1.0.37September 18, 2026

  • Restore Purchases is a button you can find. App Review rejected iOS 1.0.10 (Guideline 3.1.1, 17 Sep 2026) for having no distinct "Restore Purchases" control: Vellen's was a quiet text link under the Unlock button, gone once the device was licensed. It is a real button now, on the unlock screen and in Settings → About, in every state — and it says what the store answered, whether that is "restored", "your free trial is on record" or "nothing found".
  • Ask AI no longer claims a note you own doesn't exist. When nothing in your notes matched the words of a question, the assistant was told to say so and, in the same breath, forbidden to name any note — and what came out was "None of your notes explain wiki links", a claim about your notes from a model that had read none of them. It now speaks about the search ("nothing in your notes matched those words"), may point at a likely title from the list it is given, and the answer carries a label saying it came from general knowledge, not your notes (Android pass, 16 Sep 2026, Finding 1).
  • A local AI server that refuses is described as what it is. The chat reported a local server's 403 as "Your AI key was rejected — update it in Settings" — the Local provider has an address, not a key, and Settings a panel away was already saying the true thing. The chat now names the server, quotes what it said, and for a 403 explains OLLAMA_ORIGINS; any other error from a local server points at the model and the address (local-ai pass, 17 Sep 2026, Findings 1 and 2).
  • The whiteboard has Undo. A card moved, resized, added, removed or recoloured, a tree tidied — every change to a board can be taken back and redone, with ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y) or the toolbar's new Undo and Redo buttons, which is what the phone needed: a card nudged by a finger reaching for a pinch was moved for good, and nothing on the board could put it back (iOS pass, 16 Sep 2026, Finding 4). A drag is one edit however far it went; a card's text is still the usual text undo while it is open.
  • A replace across notes no longer fills the vault's synced history. The copy Vellen keeps of each note before a replace, a merge or a conflict resolution was a named version — synced with the vault and kept forever — so five vault-wide replaces on a 3,000-note vault left 48 MB of history beside 23 MB of notes, uploaded to Drive, with no way to reclaim it. Those copies are now safety copies: still listed in History with their label, but kept on this device only and pruned (the newest 20 per note, 90 days). A vault-wide replace also records them in one write instead of one per note. Copies made before this version stay where they are (big-vault pass, 17 Sep 2026, Finding 3).
  • The Undo after a replace across many notes stays until you dismiss it. It used to fade after twelve seconds — a 2,750-note run takes longer than that to look away from — and a click anywhere on the message, including one aimed at Undo that landed beside it, threw the Undo away. Any message with a button now keeps standing when the text is clicked and has its own × instead; the one after a many-note replace also waits to be dismissed rather than fading (big-vault pass, 17 Sep 2026, Finding 1).
  • Find and replace, from the keyboard. Closing the dialog put focus back at the top of the app instead of on the button that opened it — the one dialog with a field that focuses itself, which was being mistaken for the opener. And the Undo in the message afterwards was nineteen Tab stops away with twelve seconds to get there. Now focus returns to the button; while a message with Undo is showing, the next Tab from anywhere goes straight to it (once — Shift-Tab goes back, and the order is normal again); and a message never fades while the pointer is over it or the keyboard is in it (a11y pass, 17 Sep 2026, Finding 1).
  • Tapping a link in a note follows it on iPhone and iPad. A finger tap on a [[link]] only ever placed the caret in it: in editable text, iOS takes the tap for itself and never sends the click the editor was listening for. The tap itself is read now, from the touch — one finger, down and up in place — with the same rule as a click: it follows, unless the caret was already on that line, in which case you're editing it. A swipe still scrolls and a long press still selects (iOS pass, 16 Sep 2026, Finding 3).
  • "Replace in all notes" no longer leaves a half-done run behind. When one note could not be written (its folder read-only, say), the notes before it in the list were rewritten and nothing said so — no message, no Undo, and the dialog recounted as if the run had never happened (files pass, 17 Sep 2026). Now the notes already rewritten are put back, the run is refused, and the message names the note and the reason. The Undo afterwards also reports a note it could not put back instead of stopping.
  • On a phone, --- and two spaces reach the file as typed. The smart-dash guard from v1.0.35 was built on a guess about what the iOS keyboard sends. Recordings of the real events showed three things: the keyboard deletes the hyphen and then sends the em dash as a plain character (so --- was still saved as —-, and tables, frontmatter and horizontal rules could not be typed); WebKit keeps a trailing space as a no-break space until it inserts the next character itself (the 0xA0 that reached files); and the second of two spaces arrives as a space replacing the first (so two keystrokes became one and a nested list could not be indented by hand). Now a dash right after that delete is the -- typed, the lost second space is put back once WebKit is done, and text leaves the editor with every no-break space made plain — the editor itself is left holding whatever WebKit wrote, because rewriting it made WebKit drop the spaces (iOS pass, 16 Sep 2026, Findings 1 and 2). A no-break space pasted into a note on a phone is made plain too. The recorder stays in the code for the next such hunt: a build made with VITE_INPUT_LOG=1 writes every keyboard event to input-log.md in the vault.
  • Vellen launches on iOS 27. Any build made with Xcode 27 was terminated by iOS 27 at launch for not adopting the UIScene life cycle — found by the new-iOS pass on its first run, before anything had shipped that way (the 1.0.10 in the store was built with the iOS 26 SDK and is unaffected). The app now declares the scene manifest, and the windowing library it uses carries a one-line fix (a freed scene configuration) until tauri picks up the version that has it. Known and minor: on an iPad, asking for a second Vellen window may open a blank one.
  • The Mac download builds again on Xcode 27. Packaging the disk image failed at the step that arranges its window in Finder; that step is now skipped, so the image opens with a plain window. The app inside is unchanged and still signed, notarized and stapled. A disk image left behind by an interrupted build no longer stops the next one.

Version 1.0.35September 15, 2026

Round two (15 Sep 2026, v1.0.34): the fixes, tiered per READINESS.md.

  • Desktop "Sign in with Google" works again for anyone not already signed in. Since the 4 Sep hardening, Vellen's /ping refused any page origin — including the app's own health check — so sign-in reported "another program is answering on port 21847" and never opened the browser. That program was Vellen. The check now sends no page origin and treats the server's own refusal as proof it is Vellen (sync pass, 15 Sep 2026, Finding 1, Tier 1). The port sentence no longer has "check your connection" glued after it.
  • iOS no longer rewrites what you type. --- reached the disk as —- and "straight" as “straight” with auto-correct off, so frontmatter, tables and horizontal rules could not be typed on a phone (iOS pass, Finding 1, Tier 1). The Mac guard listened only for insertReplacementText; the phone keyboard hands over the curly quote as the typed character and an em dash replacing the hyphen before it. Both are declined on touch platforms and the typed characters put in; an em dash typed on its own (long-press) stays.
  • Find and replace across a big folder finishes, shows progress, and really stops. Every named version used to re-read every earlier version's meta file — the Nth note cost N reads, 2,750 notes ran 53 minutes without changing one, and Cancel closed the dialog while the writes went on (big-vault pass, Finding 1). A named version is now one blob and one meta file; the dialog counts "Saving a version of each note first — 120 of 2,750…" then "Replacing — …"; Stop before any rewrite leaves everything untouched, Stop during keeps what was done, says how many, and Undo covers exactly those.
  • Tab on a whiteboard moves focus for people arriving by keyboard. Walking a board with Tab made a card per press (26 Tabs, 26 cards on disk), and the handler kept firing under an open Settings dialog (a11y pass, Finding 1). A card reached by Tab-walking lets Tab and Enter do what they do everywhere; a card selected by pointer keeps the outliner keys; nothing fires while a dialog or menu is open.
  • One double-tap makes one card on Android. Its WebView synthesises a dblclick from the two taps (iOS's doesn't), so both paths made a card, stacked (Android pass, Finding 2).
  • A note that comes back from a delete/edit clash appears at once, in the list and in search — it was written to disk and never indexed until a relaunch (sync pass, Finding 2).
  • A case-only rename (final → FINAL) leaves one row, from Finder or from Vellen; a note replaced by a folder of the same name drops out of the list. The watcher asked the disk "does the old path exist", and a case-insensitive volume said yes — one phantom row per rename, the note listed as its own unlinked mention (files pass, Findings 1 and 3). It now checks for a file under that exact name.
  • "Not saved — the last save failed" in the footer while a save is failing, instead of "Saving…" (files pass, Finding 2 — the twin of the v1.0.33 conflict fix).
  • Ask AI finds a note you name, and never describes one it wasn't shown. "What does the note Linking your notes say?" sent no vault text (the relevance floor dropped the note it named) and the answer invented a title and contents; "List the titles of the notes in my vault" got "no matching notes" (Android pass, Finding 1, every platform). A title spelled out in the question is retrieved whatever the floor says; a question about what notes exist gets the folder's titles as context; and when nothing matched, the prompt says so in words the model can't read past.
  • The iOS trial sheet, once dismissed, stays dismissed. The Swift trial function had no cancel branch, so cancelling read as "couldn't tell", the "won't ask again" reassurance never showed, and the sheet came back every launch; on a device with no Apple Account the dismissal surfaces as a thrown "User cancelled" error, now recognised too (reviewer pass, Finding 1). The sentence explaining the sheet stays on screen until the sheet is dealt with instead of fading under it, and no longer sits behind the "Pulling your notes from Drive…" banner (Finding 3). The banner itself is a bar across a phone's bottom again, not a narrow column over the note (sync pass, Finding 6).
  • Enter on an empty checklist item ends the list in one press, on every platform. It took three, and left a stray blank line, because the Markdown keymap treated [ ] as content — and was registered twice, once at a precedence nothing else could beat (iOS pass, Finding 3).
  • The phone's frame no longer slides up under the Dynamic Island while typing in a table cell, a whiteboard card or the AI sheet: the frame is sized to the visible height while the keyboard is up, and any scroll the engine puts on it is undone at once (iOS pass, Finding 2).
  • A local model name is matched without regard to capitals — "Qwen3:8b" was refused with "Installed: qwen3:8b" — and the model and address fields no longer accept a phone keyboard's capital or an autofill (local-ai pass, Finding 1). On a phone the address field's example is the shape that can work, not localhost (Android pass, Finding 8).
  • A phone draws the 600 most-linked notes of a big folder, not all 3,000. Unticking "Current note only" on a 3,000-note folder took 68 s, went blank for most of it and landed as an unreadable blob (big-vault pass, Finding 2). Above 600 notes a phone now keeps the most-connected ones — the hubs the picture is about — and the toolbar says "the 600 most-linked of 3,005 notes … a phone doesn't draw them all"; a Mac, iPad or PC still draws everything. The neighbourhood notice says what unticking will show.
  • Help caught up with the last three releases (Robert, 16 Sep 2026: "have we been keeping the help up to date?"): the search box's ✕; Enter on an empty item ending a list; how Ask AI treats a note you name, a question about what notes exist, and a question nothing matches; the "From Vellen Help" chips; model names without regard to capitals and the "answered by" line; sync history rows for a sync that didn't finish or a note that couldn't be copied; the footer's two "Not saved" states; the trial sheet on a device with no Apple Account; symlinked notes; the phone graph's cap.
  • Polish from the same round. Touch: the [[ picker's rows truncate with an ellipsis again and its title and path have a gap between them; the outline picker / command palette rows, the drawer's Views rows and Settings' Test connection / Disconnect meet the 44 pt floor, and on Android every floor is 48 dp; the Tasks due button and the whiteboard tips' ✕ take a finger-sized tap; connector dots on a zoomed-out phone board sit outside the card instead of over its text; the phone's frame and notices keep clear of the keyboard. Phones: every Settings row with a sentence stacks its buttons underneath (the sync paragraph, the "is connected" line, the encryption unlock label were one word per line); the note title in the header follows the system text size; the EULA re-flows instead of keeping its source line breaks; "On My iPhone" no longer breaks mid-word; the Tables empty state says how to set one up here (long-press a folder → Table view) instead of sending you to a Mac. Words: "this device" in the encryption intro and the recovery kit; a branch note's heading follows the name the file was given; "Refresh model list" counts what the server has, not the built-in presets; the chat says which model answered when the server used a different one; "From Vellen Help" chips name a topic only when the answer really drew on it (no more "All keyboard shortcuts" under an essay), and a request to write something attaches no manual at all. Assistive tech: "Add card to To do", "Collapse Doing", "Set a due date for …" instead of three identical names; the Help search box has a name; a tab's close × is no longer a control nested inside the tab; disclosure triangles are not spoken. Files: a symlinked note says once that edits are saved as a copy in the folder. The calendar's "+N more" stays inside its cell in a short window.
  • A sync that fails leaves a history row ("Didn't finish — Google Drive had a problem on its side — nothing was changed; it will be tried again") instead of a red bar that goes away, and Google's "Internal Error" is worded as Vellen's; a note that couldn't be copied is said in the status line and the history rather than "Already up to date" (sync pass, Finding 3).
  • Builds on Xcode 27. Its Swift Package Manager compiled the billing package's iOS build against the macOS SDK and hid the functions Rust calls; swift-rs 1.0.8 handles both. The Mac needs rustup component add llvm-tools once — ship.sh and the pass preflight check for it.

Version 1.0.34September 15, 2026

  • Find and replace has a button in the note's header ("ab→", beside the pin) and a choice of This note or All notes. Stripping the colons out of a 10:23:05:23:07:31:0A in the note you're editing is the everyday case (Robert, 15 Sep 2026), and it belongs in the same dialog as the vault-wide replace rather than only behind ⌘F. The header button opens on This note; the command palette's "Find and replace in all notes…" opens on All notes; either can be switched at the top. Both scopes get the same protection — the note is saved to History first, the message afterwards has Undo — and the button says what it will do: "Replace in “Ids”", "Remove in 4 notes". The phone gets the header button and a "Find and replace in this note…" command, one note only.
  • "Replace in all notes…" is a command with its own dialog, not a row under the search box. The row was one tap away on every platform, took the search box's text verbatim (so tag:work listed the notes tagged work while Replace hunted for those literal characters), and its dialog promised that every note was "covered by backups" while it took no version of anything — a note never opened in Vellen had no history at all, and its old wording was simply gone (Robert, 15 Sep 2026: "more dangerous than useful"). Now, on a keyboard layout, the command palette's "Replace in all notes…" opens a dialog with Find and Replace with, a live count of exactly what it would touch, and a button that says the number ("Replace in 4 notes"; "Remove in 4 notes" when Replace with is empty). Before any note is rewritten, every note gets a named version ("Before replacing X with Y") — and if one of those can't be saved, nothing is replaced, as with restores and sync conflicts. Afterwards the message has Undo, which puts each note back unless it has been edited again since. The phone has no vault-wide replace at all (see the entry above for what it has); the sidebar search box only searches.
  • A ✕ in the search box empties it in one tap — results and the replace row go with it, and the cursor stays in the box for the next search. Escape still does the same on a keyboard; on a phone there is no Escape, and the only way to put a search away was to backspace the words out one by one (Robert, 15 Sep 2026). Finger-sized on touch.

Version 1.0.32September 14, 2026

Everything below comes from the first full unattended round of hands-on
passes (14 Sep 2026: iOS, Android, reviewer, sync, local AI, big vault,
files, accessibility — 61 findings, reports/passes/2026-09-14-*.md).

  • A note that isn't UTF-8 text can no longer be destroyed by typing in it. A .md in an old code page (the kind an imported Windows vault is full of) showed � for every accented letter, and the first autosave wrote those replacement characters over the original bytes for good, with no version kept. Such a note now opens read-only with a banner saying why; Edit anyway keeps a byte-for-byte copy under .history/raw/ first.
  • Reinstalling on Android and choosing the same folder opens your notes again instead of creating an empty "My notes (1)" beside them — which showed the welcome note and none of your writing, with nothing to say why. The drawer's item is now "Open a folder as a notes folder…".
  • Local AI works from the built Android app. Requests to a local server carried the app's own origin, which Ollama refuses (403) — and the refusal was reported as "can't reach the server". Local requests now send no origin, and a server that answers no is described as refusing, with the status.
  • Diagnostics no longer leak a word of a note's name. A three-word file name in an error left its middle word between the redaction markers; the whole path goes now, spaces and all.
  • Android's Back button closes what is on top first: an open ⋯ menu or the drawer, before anything behind them moves — and it keeps working after the app has been sent to the background and brought back (every press used to quit the app from then on).
  • iPhone and iPad: the app no longer scrolls out from under the status bar on a swipe, and tapping a field no longer zooms the page and leaves it zoomed (the "Connect" button, the encryption unlock screen and the note-details ✕ all went off-screen that way). Fields keep at least 16 px text on touch screens; pinch-zoom is unchanged.
  • Menus fit the phone. The whiteboard card menu ran past the right edge with "Remove from board (the note itself is untouched)" cut at "unto…"; menus now wrap within the screen.
  • A folder renamed or moved in Finder is followed. Its notes used to stay listed under the old name, unopenable ("may still be downloading"), and then appear twice once anything touched them. A note renamed in Finder while open keeps its tab too, with a notice, instead of the tab closing without a word.
  • Sync says the right thing after a delete/edit clash. "1 needs your review (copies saved)" over a note that simply came back — nothing to review, no copy saved — is now "1 note you deleted came back, edited elsewhere"; that sync shows in the recent list rather than being filed as quiet; and the conflict copy an edit/edit clash creates is uploaded by the same sync, not the next one.
  • Vellen's AI can answer "what format are my notes in, and where do they live?" A Help topic, Where your notes are stored — plain Markdown files, now exists for it to read (it used to search the web and conclude Vellen might not exist).
  • Branch → note never makes a second note with an existing title (which made every [[link]] to it ambiguous); it numbers the new one and says so.
  • Boards say when notes are off the columns: "3 without a status →" beside the count, linking to the "No status" column — a new board over a folder read "4 notes" above three empty columns with the fourth column off the edge of an iPad.
  • Calendar cells show a few chips and "+N more" instead of crushing eighteen items into 6-pixel slivers.
  • The graph on a phone shows its screen and progress at once rather than a blank page for the 25 seconds a 3,000-note graph took to build, and the finished layout is framed in the viewport instead of a corner. Tidy tree says when a tree is taller than the screen and starts at its top.
  • Touch targets meet the 44-point floor on phones and tablets — the note action row (Delete no longer one of five identical circles), the calendar's month arrows, the conflict banner's buttons (which now wrap instead of running off the screen), the [[ note picker, AI provider chips, the whiteboard toolbar; checkboxes grow with the text size.
  • Phone header names the view — Tasks, Graph, Calendar — instead of the note you left; search excerpts read as prose, not Markdown source; Help paragraphs re-flow on a phone; the whiteboard's gesture hint wraps instead of stopping mid-word; Android's first frame is dark, not white.
  • Local AI wording. The green headline names the model that is actually answering (LM Studio uses whatever it has loaded); "Refresh model list" works on LM Studio; the row under an amber "couldn't be verified" no longer says "is connected"; a settled model name is said once; small models no longer echo "[Note]" from the citation example.
  • Screen readers: the notes list is a named tree that announces the note you arrow to; the note editor is "Note text"; every property field is named by its property and every Remove says what it removes; the Settings, note-panel and saved-view tab lists move with the arrow keys and take one Tab stop; whiteboard and board cards are reachable by keyboard; a note's tab is named for the note alone; the four sidebar "+" buttons say what they add; "Links (2)".
  • Store screen: "Vellen couldn't reach the App Store just now — your trial is unaffected" replaces "The App Store record: couldn't be read just now."; the unlock covers "your iPhone, iPad and Mac" rather than "this store's ecosystem"; encryption settings say "this device" on a phone; a web-search answer's continuation no longer runs into the previous sentence.
  • Open a particular folder at launch — open -a Vellen --args --vault <folder> on a Mac (or --vault=<folder>) opens that folder instead of the remembered one. For scripted and automated use; it becomes the remembered folder like any other.
  • Pass infrastructure: the preflight checks the Anthropic key is live, refuses an installed app too old for --vault, and reads reachability by status; a round builds the Mac bundle from HEAD first and can be resumed; each pass frees port 1420, restarts the Simulator app, boots the named devices and tears everything down after; the Android pass's spurious conflict banner (a resume rescan reading a half-written file) is fixed by never reading a note while our own write is in flight.

Version 1.0.30September 13, 2026

  • An outside edit that lands right after an autosave is no longer missed. For two seconds after Vellen saved a note it treated any change to that file as the echo of its own write and ignored it — so a line appended by another program (or another device's sync) inside that window never reached the editor, no banner appeared, and the next keystrokes were refused by the safety check with nothing on screen to say why. The file's content now decides what is Vellen's own: an outside change shows at once on a clean note and raises the "changed outside this window" banner on one with unsaved typing. A file that vanishes under unsaved typing is likewise noticed whatever the clock says, and the words are kept. Failed-create notices name the note without its extension.
  • Opening a big vault: the folder listing is one round trip. The timings shipped in v1.0.29 answered the question they were built for: of a 3,000-note vault's 2.4 s open, 1.07 s was "scanning the folder" — not disk time (27 ms in one process) but three thousand crossings of the webview boundary, one per note, to read its date and size. The app now walks the vault natively and answers once; the old walk stays as the fallback. A second line in the timings, "launch: webview start → open vault begins", now separates app start-up from the vault open, since the 5.9 s the pass saw from launch held only 2.4 s of the latter.

Version 1.0.29September 13, 2026

  • The graph settles in about half the time on a big vault. A 3,000-note, 15,000-link vault took seven seconds to arrange. The physics is unchanged; its bookkeeping was the cost — a fresh hash map of neighbour buckets every tick and every pair visited twice. It now sorts nodes into a grid of reused typed arrays and sees each pair once, with a tighter neighbourhood that still covers the spacing unlinked notes settle at; and the dots are drawn as one shape rather than three thousand. Measured: 8.7 → 4.4 ms a step.
  • Vellen now times its own slow phases, and the diagnostics say so. Opening a vault (each step and the total), arranging the graph, a search, collecting tasks and Tidy tree each record how long they took; Settings → About → Copy diagnostics includes a "Timings (this session)" section. A hands-on pass spent a day trying to time a release build from outside with screenshots and got numbers that were plausible and wrong; this is the answer from inside.
  • Big-vault housekeeping. Opening a note re-rendered every row of the sidebar (600 ms at 3,000 notes — a row now redraws only when the change names it or something below it); the hover-only delete button is created only for the hovered row (a third of the sidebar's DOM); and the indexing progress line updates ten times a second instead of once per fifty files, which on a cached open re-rendered the app sixty times for a line nobody could see.

Version 1.0.28September 13, 2026

Found on a real iPhone within an hour of shipping, on the exact test written to close it.
